Higher-fidelity than a mechanism sketch, still a click-through wireframe, not a finished visual system. All navigation is CSS-only (radio buttons), no JavaScript, matching the rest of this repo's mocks. Shepherd is shown living inside a broader platform catalog rather than as a standalone tool, deliberately: the safety story (read-only business metadata, confirm-then-compose, human-only merge) holds together better when it's one governed action among several, not a special case. The fixed-constraints panel is never editable anywhere it appears, catalog entity page included, it's read from business metadata, while "here's what I understood" is what Shepherd inferred and exactly what a developer can correct.
