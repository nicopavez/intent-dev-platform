Low-fidelity wireframe, not a visual design. Grayscale intentional: this is a mechanism sketch, not a brand exercise. Two panels are deliberately separate: the fixed constraints panel is never editable here, it's read from business metadata, while the "here's what I understood" panel is the developer's own description and is exactly what she can correct. The confirm step is the safety checkpoint, not the input format: both panels appear whether the source was this blurb, a POC repo, or a legacy repo.
