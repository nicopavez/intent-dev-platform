Low-fidelity wireframe, not a finished CLI. Click [1]/[2]/[3] above to switch input modes, same mechanism underneath in all three: extraction is a draft, the context bar (tier/budget/owner/region) is never editable from any of them, a conflict is named and never silently resolved, and composition only ever draws from pre-approved modules. The context bar is the k9s-style touch on purpose: business constraints stay visible the entire session, not just at confirm time.
